# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want HealthyWork to be a project where anyone can take part without being harassed, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are contributing in a second language or for the first time.
- Assume good intent, and say clearly when something is not working for you.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not about the person.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
- Insults, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

In every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/yagopereiradev). Reports are read, taken seriously and kept confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainer will review the report and respond in a way that fits what happened: a private conversation and a request to change the behaviour, a formal warning, a temporary ban from project spaces, or a permanent one.
